HohoSide Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

HohoSide is a browser extension for keeping your own notes, tasks and web clips in the side panel. Our principle is simple: your data belongs to you and stays on your computer.

What we collect

Nothing. HohoSide has no server and no accounts, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Where your data lives

  • Notes, tasks, clips and settings are kept in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • Images, note version history and automatic snapshots are kept in your browser's local IndexedDB.
  • If you turn on “Sync to a local folder”, notes are written as Markdown files to a folder you choose on your computer.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes this data from the browser; folders you synced to and files you exported are not affected.

Network requests

HohoSide only uses the network when you ask it to:

  • When you clip an image from a web page, the image is downloaded from the site it is on and stored locally so it works offline.
  • When you open a clip's original page, the page opens in your browser.

These requests go only to the sites the content came from. None of your data is sent to us or to any third party.

Permissions

Each permission is used only for its feature; see the permission notes on the store page. Optional permissions (reading the current tab, access to a site) are requested the first time you use the related feature. You can decline them, and revoke them at any time in Chrome's extension settings.

Your control

  • Export all your data (Markdown or JSON) at any time.
  • Restore from automatic snapshots, or remove the extension to delete all data from the browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the effective date.

Contact

For questions, email ryan.tian.cn@gmail.com or open an issue at https://github.com/hoho-work/hohoside/issues.

HohoSide 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 4 日

HohoSide 是一个浏览器扩展，用来在侧边栏里记录你自己的笔记、待办和网页摘录。我们的原则很简单：你的数据只属于你，只存在你的电脑上。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。 HohoSide 没有服务器，不需要账号，不包含任何统计、分析、广告或追踪代码。

数据存在哪里

  • 笔记、待办、摘录和设置保存在你的浏览器本地存储（chrome.storage.local）中。
  • 图片、笔记的历史版本和自动快照保存在浏览器本地的 IndexedDB 中。
  • 如果你开启了「同步到本地文件夹」，笔记会以 Markdown 文件写入你自己选择的电脑文件夹。
  • 卸载扩展会删除浏览器中的这些数据；同步到文件夹或导出的文件不受影响。

网络请求

HohoSide 只在以下由你主动触发的情况下访问网络：

  • 摘录网页图片时：从图片所在的网站下载这张图片并保存在本机，以便离线查看。
  • 打开摘录的原网页时：在浏览器中打开该网页。

这些请求只发往内容本来所在的网站，不会把你的任何数据发送给我们或第三方。

权限

每项权限都只用于其功能本身，具体见商店页面的权限说明。可选权限（读取当前标签页、访问某个网站）只在你第一次使用相关功能时请求，你可以拒绝，也可以随时在 Chrome 的扩展设置中撤销。

你的控制权

  • 随时导出全部数据（Markdown 或 JSON）。
  • 随时从自动快照恢复，或删除扩展以清除浏览器中的所有数据。

变更

如本政策有变化，会在此页面更新并修改生效日期。

联系

如有问题，请发邮件至 ryan.tian.cn@gmail.com，或在 https://github.com/hoho-work/hohoside/issues 提交反馈。
